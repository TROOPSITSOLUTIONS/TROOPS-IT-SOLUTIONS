    ABOUT TEAM BCT

    The digital sentinels of the nation. Safeguarding cyber borders, fighting propaganda, and serving humanity.

    Welcome to Bangladesh Cyber Tribe (Team BCT). Our journey began in 2024 with only 11 highly dedicated members during a time of extreme national crisis. Formed strictly out of necessity to protect our digital sovereignty, this brotherhood has evolved massively. Today, in 2026, we are operating with renewed energy, superior intelligence, and an unyielding commitment to our motherland. We are not just a group; we are the system that protects the truth.

    Deen, Desh & Islam

    Our ultimate driving force. Every line of code we write, every operation we execute, is strictly rooted in the protection of our Deen (Faith), our beloved homeland Bangladesh, and the sacred values of Islam. We strive for a cyberspace completely free from atheism, obscenity, and defamation. Over the years, we have successfully neutralized thousands of blasphemous contents and dismantled massive gambling syndicates.

    Vanguard of July

    Politically and morally, we proudly stand as the digital frontline of the historic July Revolution. We fiercely protect the mandate of the people, expose fascist remnants hidden within the system, and stand firmly with the indomitable spirit of the revolution that reshaped our nation. We honor the blood of our martyrs by continuously defending the truth online.

    Crime Data & OSINT Analysis

    Team BCT functions as an elite cyber intelligence hub. We do not merely react; we analyze, trace, and predict. Our highly specialized units continuously monitor domestic crime rates, perform deep Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) operations, trace cyber-criminal footprints, and analyze vast datasets to expose hidden illegal syndicates operating within and outside Bangladesh.

    Anti-Bhagwa Defense

    We operate as a relentless shield against Indian expansionist agendas and anti-state propaganda. We actively dismantle foreign networks that spread targeted misinformation, attempt to destabilize our national sovereignty, or attack our cultural integrity. Any cyber aggression against Bangladesh is met with a massive, coordinated counter-offensive.

    Humanitarian Action

    Our duty extends far beyond the computer terminal. Team BCT actively engages in real-world charity and social welfare. From providing immediate disaster relief during national emergencies to supporting financially struggling families and standing beside the oppressed, we ensure our physical impact matches our powerful digital presence.

    Trustiq & Cyber Ethics

    Through Trustiq, our dedicated technological and educational division, we provide elite digital infrastructure and web solutions. Our operations adhere to strict ethical guidelines, focusing heavily on defensive cybersecurity, raising cyber awareness among the youth, and building robust, impenetrable systems for a safer digital ecosystem.
